## Best Practices for LinkedIn Posts

### Timing
- Post on Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM or 12-1 PM
- Avoid Mondays and weekends

### Hashtags
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags
- Mix popular (#DataScience) and niche (#Streamlit)
- Place at the end of post

### Engagement
- Ask a question at the end
- Respond to all comments within 1-2 hours
- Tag relevant people/companies (if appropriate)

### Visuals
- Use high-quality screenshots
- Keep text in images minimal
- Ensure mobile-friendly viewing

### Call-to-Action
- Include GitHub link
- Invite people to try the tool
- Ask for feedback
